============================================================================================
 WHAT HAS BEEN ACHIEVED
============================================================================================

--- V1.0  "Main Release" ---------------------------------------- [RELEASED]  June 2026

The first public version: a virtual First Officer for the ToLiss A319, A320/A20N and
A321/A21N.

    - Performs the normal procedures of a complete flight, from the preliminary cockpit
      preparation to parking, on your command or automatically
    - Reads the checklists with you and verifies that every item is really set
    - Monitors the flight and makes the standard callouts during takeoff, flight and landing
    - One engine taxi for departure and arrival
    - RNP AR and ILS CAT II/III operations
    - Briefing page for takeoff and landing, and a main page to follow the flight
    - Keyboard commands for flaps, gear, checklist response and the interface
    - Two voice packs: Owen and Adriana


--- V1.1  "Procedures online" ----------------------------------- [RELEASED]  August 2026

Fly with the SOP you choose.

    - Swappable procedure and checklist packs: fly with the Airbus standard or with an
      airline's SOP (Avianca 2022)
    - The FO requests the weather over datalink (Hoppie) and sets the altimeter, or you
      simply type the QNH
    - Briefings fill in automatically from the MCDU and can be changed after confirming them
    - Session recovery: continue your flight where you left it after a crash or a reload
    - New checklists (Cockpit Preparation, Taxi, Departure Change, Line Up) and a new
      Taxi procedure
    - Rebuilt audio: callouts sound inside the cockpit and no longer talk over each other


============================================================================================
 WHAT IS BEING BUILT NOW
============================================================================================

--- V1.2 (working title) ---------------------------------------- [IN DEVELOPMENT]

A cleaner and stronger base before the fleet grows.

    - Checklists on screen: optionally follow each item and its answer as the FO reads it
    - Checklists and procedures rebuilt from the inside: lighter, more reliable and ready
      to welcome new aircraft and new SOP packs
    - More natural callouts: the FO confirms the takeoff runway at line up
      ("two seven left") and says "QNH" or "ALTIMETER" depending on the unit in use
    - Creating a voice pack is much easier: callout timing is taken from the audio files
    - Session recovery also resumes a one engine taxi exactly where it was
    - The old "Legacy" pack is retired, Airbus and Avianca 2022 remain
